**Visor** is a free, open-source Mac app by **Apurva Mukherjee** that turns the notch on your MacBook into something you can use.<br>Your music, your calendar, a shelf for files and the volume and brightness HUDs, right where the camera sits.

<td width="50%" valign="top">
<h3><img src="assets/readme/icons/music.svg" width="40" align="center"> &nbsp;Now Playing</h3>
Cover art, title, artist, a timeline you can scrub and the usual controls for whatever is playing. Any app that reports to macOS works, including YouTube Music in Chrome or Safari. Apple Music, Spotify or a YouTube Music desktop app can be the source instead.

<td width="50%" valign="top">

With the notch closed, the cover sits on one side and a visualizer bounces on the other. Sneak peek flashes the new track when the song changes, and lyrics can run under the artist name (beta). Bring your own Lottie animation for the visualizer.

<tr>

Trade the cover for a spinning record with the art on its label. The tonearm drops when you hit play and swings back when you pause. Settings → Media → Vinyl mode.
</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">
<h3><img src="assets/readme/icons/lock.svg" width="40" align="center"> &nbsp;Lock &amp; unlock</h3>
Lock or unlock your Mac and a padlock snaps shut or springs open in the notch for three seconds, then fades back to what's playing.

<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">
<h3><img src="assets/readme/icons/hud.svg" width="40" align="center"> &nbsp;HUDs</h3>
Volume, display brightness and keyboard backlight show up in the notch instead of the big macOS overlay. Pick the classic or inline style, show the percentage, add a glow or tint the bar with your accent color.
</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">
<h3><img src="assets/readme/icons/battery.svg" width="40" align="center"> &nbsp;Battery</h3>
Charge level and percentage next to the notch, plus a heads-up when you plug in or unplug.

<td width="50%" valign="top">

A week strip with today's events and reminders beside the player. Choose which calendars show, hide all-day events or finished reminders, and let it scroll to what's next.

</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

## <img src="assets/readme/icons/download.svg" width="34" align="center"> &nbsp;Install

1. Download the latest `.dmg` from **[Releases](https://github.com/apurvamukherjee/visor-mac-island/releases/latest)**. Every build ever made also lives in [`new-releases/`](new-releases).
2. Open it and drag **Visor** into **Applications**.
3. Builds are ad-hoc signed, so macOS quarantines them. Clear that once:

   ```bash
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Visor.app
   ```

4. Open Visor. The welcome tour explains each permission before it asks for it.

What changed in each version: [`CHANGELOG.md`](CHANGELOG.md).

## <img src="assets/readme/icons/gestures.svg" width="34" align="center"> &nbsp;How to use

| Do this | And Visor |
| --- | --- |
| Hover over the notch, or click it | opens |
| Move the pointer away | closes |
| Scroll down on the notch with two fingers | opens |
| Scroll up on the open notch | closes |
| Drag a file toward the notch | opens the shelf for you to drop it |
| Click the record icon in the menu bar | shows Settings, Restart and Quit |

## <img src="assets/readme/icons/keyboard.svg" width="34" align="center"> &nbsp;Shortcuts

| Keys | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd> | Open or close the notch |
| <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>H</kbd> | Sneak peek at what's playing |

Change either one in Settings → Shortcuts.

## <img src="assets/readme/icons/shield.svg" width="34" align="center"> &nbsp;Permissions

| Permission | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Accessibility | Replacing the system HUDs and handling the brightness and backlight keys |
| Calendars, Reminders | The calendar and reminders beside the player |
| Camera | The mirror |
| Automation | Controlling Apple Music and Spotify |

Each one is optional. Skip it and only the feature that needs it stays off.

## <img src="assets/readme/icons/chip.svg" width="34" align="center"> &nbsp;Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later
- Apple silicon
- A MacBook with a notch for the full effect (other displays get a floating island)

## <img src="assets/readme/icons/terminal.svg" width="34" align="center"> &nbsp;Build from source

```bash
brew install xcodegen
xcodegen generate
xcodebuild -scheme Visor -configuration Debug build
```

Package a styled `.dmg`:

```bash
bash scripts/make-dmg.sh
```
